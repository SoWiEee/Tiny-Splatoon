# Kraken source contract

This contract is captured from the reference `Actor` and `SpecialSystem` implementations, not inferred from `SPECIALS.kraken` configuration alone. The reproducible harness is [export-kraken-source-fixtures.mjs](../tools/export-kraken-source-fixtures.mjs); its JSON snapshots and six SHA-256 source stamps are in [kraken_source_fixtures.json](../tests/kraken_source_fixtures.json), with compact constants in [kraken_source_fixtures.hpp](../tests/kraken_source_fixtures.hpp). Run `node tools/export-kraken-source-fixtures.mjs --check` to verify the committed fixture against the current source.

The source configuration is duration 7 s, horizontal speed 7.2 m/s, hop velocity 10.5 m/s, grounded launch velocity 8.5 m/s, forward launch addition 3.25 m/s (`attackFwd * 0.5`), blast radius 2.3 m, max damage 200, knock multiplier 0.02 capped at 4, attack cooldown 0.65 s, and trail paint radius 1.15 m.

## Activation and controller ownership

`Actor._startSpecial()` spends the gauge, refills ink, sets kid form, then calls `SpecialSystem.start()`. `IMPL.kraken.start()` adds `noSquid`, `inkProof`, `speed=7.2`, `accel=45`, `faceMove=true`, cooldown/attack/trail/air state; resets the normal weapon runner; hides the Character; and adds one owned Kraken body object. The source does **not** set `body=true`: Kraken remains on the normal Actor movement and weapon path. The actual activation fixture observes `special:start`, one runner reset, hidden Character, one owned object, and kid form even while squid is held.

Native actor pose needs three additional source flags for this contract: `inkProof`, `accel`, and `faceMove`. `speed`/move-speed and `noSquid` are already represented in the current integration seam. Kraken must not be represented as a body-owning special. The regular movement, jump, facing, and frame-order paths remain responsible for the actor; its main-weapon trigger is routed through Kraken's special weapon hook, which replaces ordinary firing while active.

`inkProof` affects the `onEnemy` movement/damage decision; it does not rewrite the sampled ground team. On enemy-painted ground, the fixture reports `groundTeam=2`, `onEnemy=false`, unchanged HP, and no ink damage. The grounded controller applies 45 m/s² acceleration to the 7.2 m/s target; the fixture reaches 0.75 m/s after one 1/60 s frame and 7.2 m/s by frame 15. `faceMove` makes source facing follow horizontal velocity. In air, Actor uses the special speed as the target but keeps its normal air acceleration; the 45 m/s² override is used by the grounded branch.

## Jump attack and landing

The normal `Actor.update()` order is material: movement and integration run before the weapon hook; the Kraken weapon hook runs after integration; `SpecialSystem.tick()` then observes the resulting state. A fire press with cooldown ready sets `attack=true` and cooldown to 0.65 s. From ground it adds 3.25 m/s along forward, sets vertical speed to 8.5 m/s, and marks the actor airborne. In air it sets vertical speed to `min(current, -16)` for a dive. It consumes the main-weapon trigger in both cases.

Kraken tick allows the regular jump path to execute. On the first airborne tick, when not attacking and vertical speed exceeds 3 m/s, it raises speed to at least 10.5 m/s. It tracks airborne state in `s.air`; on the transition back to grounded, if `attack` is set, it clears attack and emits `special:slam`. The checked headless trajectories land 41 total 60 Hz updates after the grounded attack trigger and 11 updates after the airborne dive trigger. Those counts include the trigger update. Frame accumulation is intentionally preserved as source IEEE arithmetic; the contract does not add an epsilon.

At impact, the Kraken special paints once at `landing + (0,0.3,0)` with radius `2.3 * 0.8 = 1.84`. It applies an area-damage callback with radius 2.3 and damage 60, then invokes the special blast helper. The helper skips same-team and dead actors, excludes targets beyond `radius + 0.3`, and requires line of sight from `center + (0,0.35,0)` to `victim.pos + (0,0.8,0)`. Damage uses 200 max, 120 min, quadratic falloff between kill radius `2.3 * 0.7 = 1.61` and radius 2.3; distances inside kill radius receive max damage. The actual-source fixture exercises a close target, a falloff target, a wall-blocked target, an out-of-range target, an ally, and a dead enemy. It observes two hit calls; the blocked, outside, ally, and dead actors receive none. The paint sink also records two ordinary attacker-turf splats caused by the two lethal victim splats. The generic combat burst must be checked against this exact center offset, LOS ray, `radius + 0.3` admission band, and quadratic max/min formula before reuse.

## Trail, incoming damage, and lifecycle

While grounded, each tick adds horizontal speed times `dt` to the trail accumulator. When it is greater than 0.5 m, it resets to zero (it does not subtract 0.5) and paints at `actor.pos + (0,0.2,0)` with radius 1.15. These source special paints use a random seed and do not charge special gauge. A 90-frame enemy-ink movement trace records the actual repeated paint calls while confirming ink proof remains active.

`Actor.damage()` rejects invulnerable hits before special filters. Otherwise Kraken's `filterDamage()` returns zero, flashes the actor, and applies knock `min(4, damage * 0.02)`. The shared knock helper pushes away from the attacker; if horizontal separation is degenerate, it pushes opposite actor yaw. A grounded victim gets vertical speed at least `1.4 + knock * 0.18` and becomes airborne. The fixture records a 100-damage hit as zero HP loss, a 2 m/s horizontal shove, 1.76 m/s vertical launch, and an invulnerability case with no shove.

At duration expiry, `SpecialSystem.tick()` first advances time and calls Kraken tick, then ends the special when `t >= 7`. With repeated 1/60 s source steps, floating-point accumulation ends on frame 421 (`7.016666…` elapsed), removes the owned visual body, restores Character visibility, clears `specialActive`, and emits `special:end` with reason `time`. On owner death, `Actor.splat()` calls `SpecialSystem.onSplat()`; Kraken end removes its body and restores visibility, after which `Actor.splat()` hides the dead Character. The fixture therefore records visibility calls `[false, true, false]`, an empty owned-object set, null active special, and an end reason of `splat`.

The harness uses headless physics, rendering/effect, and paint sinks, but executes the actual source Actor, SpecialSystem, and configuration code. It is intended to lock state/event and gameplay equations; it does not assert Three.js rendering or mesh geometry parity.
